## 🌐 How to Publish it to Hostinger (`meridiancrestlineexports.com`) in 2 Minutes

Since you already own `meridiancrestlineexports.com` on Hostinger:

1. Log in to [hostinger.com](https://hostinger.com).
2. Go to **Websites** &rarr; click **Manage** next to `meridiancrestlineexports.com`.
3. Click on **File Manager** (under Files).
4. Double-click to open the `public_html` folder.
5. Simply drag and drop all the files from this folder (`index.html`, `css`, `js`, `images`) into `public_html`.
6. That's it! Visit `https://meridiancrestlineexports.com` and your website will be live worldwide!
